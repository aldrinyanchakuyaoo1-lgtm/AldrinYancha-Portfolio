📶 Reminder: Connect to the internet first
For MindSpace to work fully, connect to the internet before opening it. You can still use it offline, but these will not show up or not work without internet:
  • Icons (Font Awesome) – icons on buttons and menus will disappear or appear blank.
  • Google Sans font – Arial will be used instead.
  • Journal post screenshot (Save as PNG) – will not work.
Everything else (notes, code editor, practice, games, and your saved data) still works offline.

About MindSpace
THE IDEA BEHIND THE APP

One idea. One space to learn.

MindSpace began with an idea from Aldrin Pagangpang Yancha, a student at Datamex College Fairview. He imagined bringing useful study tools together in one place—a space to review, stay organized, reflect, and keep learning.

That idea became MindSpace: a personal study companion designed to make everyday learning feel more organized, creative, and encouraging.

WHY IT WAS CREATED

Study tools that belong together

MindSpace was created to bring the everyday parts of studying into one welcoming space. Learners can build and practice reviewer questions, save helpful material, keep journal reflections and photos, organize notes, explore Bible verses and devotions, and take a short break with educational games—all in one app.

A LOOK INSIDE

Made for the way you study

Explore a few of the tools included in MindSpace.

PRACTICE & PROGRESS

Tester / Reviewer

Create questions, practice answers, and keep important items saved for later review.

WRITE & REFLECT

Journal / Diary

Capture thoughts, study reflections, and photos together in a single journal entry.

ORGANIZE & REFLECT

Notes & Devotions

Keep sticky notes close and collect Bible verses, chapters, and personal reflections.

SHARE & RESET

Study Feed & Break Time

Browse study posts and take a refreshing pause with educational games.

MindSpace is a student’s idea, shaped into a growing space for learning, reflection, and progress.
