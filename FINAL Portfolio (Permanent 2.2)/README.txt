Portfolio source package

Open index.html in a browser to view the portfolio.
Your saved profile, project details, and uploaded photos are in portfolio-data.json and are embedded in index.html inside the portfolioEmbeddedData block. The photos are embedded as image data, so no separate upload files are required.
The permanent snapshot takes priority over older browser-saved profile data. New edits are saved in the browser and restored after refresh.
The “Want more experience? Click Here” link at the bottom of Sticky Notes, Journal, and Reviewer opens personal-project/index.html, the page reserved for the personal HTML project. Replace that placeholder with your own project files.
The source files are included: style.css, script.js, the images folder, and personal-project/.
To add or adjust a photo in Home, About Me, My Skills, My Projects, Achievements, Future Goals, or Contact Me, open Menu and choose the matching Edit section. Choose an image, drag it in the crop window to reposition it, then use the Zoom slider or + / - buttons. Use Reposition / zoom beside an existing photo to adjust it again. Save profile changes in the browser; for a permanent ZIP, open Menu → Permanent copy → Make permanent (download ZIP). Detail/Description fields are multiline: press Enter for a new line and leave one blank line between paragraphs. The tab previews preserve line breaks and paragraph spacing. For each row, choose Original, Square (1:1), Landscape (3:2), or Portrait (2:3) for its photo. Automatic layout is applied to every profile section. A section with one card uses a full-row banner, and a section with two cards uses two equal columns. With three or more cards, short cards use three columns per row; long or multiline cards become full-row banners. After each banner, the remaining short cards continue in three-column rows, and the last row expands evenly when only one or two cards remain. On smaller screens the grid becomes narrower for readability. Banners put details on the left and a larger photo on the right; choose Top, Center, or Bottom for the image position. In the Card layout menu, choose Auto, Square card (1 column), 1.5 columns (half row), or Full-row banner for any individual detail. Move up / Move down can position a favorite banner between grid rows. Tab-photo frames follow the original portrait or landscape shape and show the full image at 100%. If an old saved crop already cut off part of the original, upload the uncropped original again.
